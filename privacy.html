Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

This policy explains what is collected when you use Teshane, what is done with it, and who it is shared with. The site is operated by [full legal / trading name + business registration number] of [full physical address].

What is collected

Account details

You sign in through Google or GitHub. From them we receive your email address and display name. We never receive or store your password for either service.

The code you submit for auditing

To audit a site and rebuild it, we receive the source code you upload or pull from GitHub, and store it alongside the results of the run. This is the most sensitive material that passes through us, so two things matter:

  • Config files and secrets (such as .env) are never sent to us at all. They stay in your browser and are placed back into your download from that same window. The model does not see them and we do not store them.
  • The code is sent to our model provider for the audit and rebuild only — see "Who it is shared with" below.

Usage information

For each run we store: the time, the source (GitHub or file), the score and findings, whether it succeeded, and technical cost and usage figures we use for pricing and monitoring.

Payment information

Your token balance and a record of your purchases are stored with us. Card details never pass through us and are never stored by us — they are given directly to Paddle in their checkout.

What it is used for

  • Running the service: auditing, rebuilding, and delivering the result to you.
  • Managing your account and balance, and showing you your run history.
  • Supporting you when you get in touch, and investigating faults.
  • Measuring costs and improving the product.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with advertisers.

Who it is shared with

We rely on the following providers, each for a defined purpose:

  • Anthropic — the language model provider that performs the audit and the rebuild. The code you submit is sent to it for that purpose.
  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage.
  • Vercel — website hosting.
  • Paddle.com Market Limited — payment processing and receipts. Paddle acts as Merchant of Record.
  • Google / GitHub — sign-in only, at your choice.

Some of these providers operate outside Israel, so your information may be processed outside it.

How long it is kept

Code and run results are kept while your account is active, so you can return to your history. You can delete a run from the dashboard at any time. Purchase records are kept for the period required by tax law.

Your rights

You may see what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, and ask for it and your account to be deleted. Write to idolahav01@gmail.com and we will handle it within two business days.

If you are in the EU or the UK, you additionally have rights under the GDPR, including data portability and the right to object to processing.

Cookies

The site uses browser storage to keep you signed in. It is not used for advertising or tracking. Paddle's checkout may set its own cookies under their policy.

Security

Access is enforced at the database level: each user can read only their own data, and balances and purchases can only be changed by the server — never from a browser. That said, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy we will change the date at the top of the page. Material changes will also be sent to you by email.
