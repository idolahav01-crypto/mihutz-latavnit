01

Who we are

"Out of the Template" (מחוץ לתבנית) is an online service that analyzes websites built with AI tools (such as Claude Code, v0, Bolt, Lovable, Cursor) and fixes the design, typographic, and technical signals that make them look generic.

The service is operated by Idola, a licensed/exempt sole-proprietor business (osek) registered in Israel.

02

What data we collect

Data you provide directly:

  • Account details — name, email address, and profile photo, via Google OAuth sign-in.
  • Your website's source code and content — ZIP files you upload, a public GitHub repository URL, or a connected GitHub OAuth integration (including a scoped access token used to read the repository).
  • Payment details — will be collected once a payment processor is selected; card numbers are never stored directly by us and are processed by the payment provider itself.
  • Support communications you send us.

Data collected automatically:

  • IP address, device identifier, browser type, and operating system.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, dashboard actions, session duration.
  • Cookies and similar identifiers (see Section 7).

Under Amendment 13 to Israel's Privacy Protection Law, IP addresses, device identifiers, and geolocation data are treated as personal data.

What we do not knowingly collect: we do not request or knowingly collect sensitive data categories (health, sexual orientation, political views, etc.). Source code you upload may unintentionally contain personal data of third parties (e.g., your own customers' details embedded in the site) — you are responsible for having the legal right to upload such content.

04

Sharing data with third parties

We share data only with service providers acting on our behalf, and never sell personal data. Our current core providers:

  • Vercel — hosting the dashboard, site, and files.
  • Anthropic API — processing your source code to detect and fix AI-generic signals, subject to Anthropic's own privacy policy and terms.
  • GitHub — reading public or connected code repositories.
  • Google — OAuth authentication.

Data may also be shared with law enforcement when legally required. Each provider is bound by a data processing agreement (DPA) requiring confidentiality and appropriate security.

05

International data transfers

Since the service serves customers worldwide, data may be transferred to and processed outside Israel (including the US and EU). Israel holds an EU adequacy decision, so transfers between Israel and the EU are generally straightforward. For transfers to other jurisdictions, we rely on contractual data protection safeguards with our providers, and, where necessary, user consent.

06

Data retention and deletion

  • Subscription cancellation only (account not closed): your uploaded files and history remain available in your account exactly as they were before cancellation.
  • Permanent account closure: uploaded source code and site files are permanently deleted from our servers as soon as closure is confirmed — they are no longer part of an active site, and there is no business or legal reason to retain them.
  • Logs and analytics data: retained up to 24 months.
  • Accounting records (once invoices are issued): retained per Israeli statutory requirements, even after account closure.
07

Cookies

We use essential cookies (service operation, authentication) that don't require consent, and analytics/marketing cookies that require explicit, active opt-in consent before activation. You can change your preferences anytime via the "Privacy Preferences" link in the site footer. Consent is valid for a maximum of 12 months and will be re-requested upon expiry or a material policy change.

08

Security

The service is currently classified at basic-to-medium security level (fewer than 10,000 records, no sensitive data). Measures in place include encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, access logging, and periodic backups.

Dashboard sign-in currently uses Google OAuth only, with no separate two-factor (2FA/MFA) layer. We plan to add an additional security layer in the future; this policy will be updated once that ships. As the user base grows beyond 10,000 records, we will adopt medium-level security requirements in full (full encryption at rest, appointment of a security officer).

09

Your rights

Under Israel's Privacy Protection Law and other applicable privacy laws (such as the GDPR for EU users), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to Israeli law's narrower scope (EU residents have a broader right to erasure).
  • Object to direct-marketing processing and unsubscribe at any time.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as it was given.

To exercise these rights, contact us at idolahav01@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10

Breach notification

In the event of a serious security incident, we will notify the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority without delay, and notify affected users if the incident may cause them significant harm, in accordance with the 2017 Security Regulations.

11

The service is not intended for use by individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.
