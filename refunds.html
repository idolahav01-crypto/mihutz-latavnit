Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

This policy applies to token purchases on Teshane (the "Service"), operated by [full legal / trading name + business registration number] of [full physical address].

What a token is

A token is a prepaid unit that lets you run an action on the Service — auditing a website, or rebuilding it. A token is spent once it has started a process that actually ran and cost us real money with our model provider.

Cancelling and getting your money back

Unspent tokens

You can cancel and receive a full refund for every token you have not spent, within 14 days of purchase. No reason required.

Spent tokens

A spent token is not refundable. The reason is substantive, not technical: once you have run an audit or a rebuild, the result was delivered to you and we have already paid an external provider for it. That is digital content supplied in full, and the right of withdrawal does not apply to it once supply has begun.

At purchase you are asked to agree that supply of the digital content begins as soon as you run an action, and that you thereby waive the right of withdrawal for tokens you spend.

Partly spent purchases

If you spent some of a package, the refund is calculated on the unspent balance, at the price per token actually paid in that package.

When we refund spent tokens anyway

These are cases where the fault is ours, and we do not intend to profit from them:

  • The process failed technically and no result was delivered — the token goes back to your balance.
  • You were charged twice for the same purchase.
  • What was delivered differs materially from what the service page described.

Contact us in these cases and we will sort it out, even after 14 days.

How to request a refund

Email idolahav01@gmail.com with the address you signed up with and the order number from your receipt. We reply within two business days, and the refund is issued within 14 days of approval, to the payment method used for the purchase.

Who takes the payment

Payments are processed by Paddle.com Market Limited, acting as Merchant of Record. Paddle is the official seller of record to the payer, issues the receipt, and is the party that actually processes the refund. You can also raise a refund request with them directly at paddle.net, though contacting us is faster.
