04

Detection, taste and precision are three different jobs.

So we never do them in one pass. Your project runs through four separate stages — each tuned for exactly one kind of work, with you as the gate in the middle.

  1. Detect — nothing gets fixed yet

    Every file is scanned against the full signal list. Each finding carries the file path, a verbatim snippet, and a confidence score. In doubt? Reported absent — a false positive here would poison everything downstream.

    deterministic pass · temperature 0 · outputs detection.json + a factual profile of your site

  2. Design — one direction, not 30 patches

    Before touching a single finding, the studio commits to one coherent design direction for your business: one brand color, one type pairing, one layout principle. Every fix is derived from it — so the result reads as one designer's work.

    creative pass · second-order clichés (dark+gold, Playfair, bento) are contractually banned as fixes

  3. You approve — the human gate

    You see the direction first, then every proposed change with its old code and new code side by side. Reject anything. Nothing moves without a yes.

    no automation here, on purpose

  4. Apply — surgical edits only

    Approved changes are applied by exact match. Class names, handlers, routes, forms and imports are preserved verbatim; each edit passes six self-checks or is reverted.

    precision pass · temperature ~0.2 · full change log per edit

  5. Verify — an independent, skeptical reviewer

    A separate QA pass that never saw the reasoning diffs every line: did anything unapproved change? Is each signal actually resolved — or just swapped for the next cliché? Fail, and the edit goes back.

    adversarial pass · temperature 0 · guilty until proven safe

05

What we will never touch

The audit changes how your site reads, never what it does. The final QA pass diffs every line against this contract:

  • Class names, IDs and data-attributes — preserved verbatim
  • Event handlers, form actions, routes and imports — untouched
  • Your logo, your photos, your content — never replaced
  • Your facts — we never invent numbers, testimonials or claims. Missing facts come back to you as questions.

Who this is for

Freelancers and agencies delivering AI-drafted sites to paying clients; small businesses whose "finished" AI site still feels off; anyone shipping to the Israeli market, where RTL and regulation add dozens of signals most tools have never heard of.

07

Questions you're probably asking

What does the scan check?

The scan measures how much your site looks like it was built by AI — visually: fonts, colors, layout, copy, accessibility and Hebrew. You get a score and a list of findings, each with a snippet from your own code. The first scan is free.

What counts as an "AI signal"?

Every small thing that repeats across machine-built sites: the Inter font, a purple gradient, three symmetric cards, a "Get Started" button, an emoji in the headline, and generic copy like "seamless" and "next-level". Each one alone is trivial — together they're what makes a site look like a template, not a real business.

Do you change my content or break features?

No. We change how the site looks, not what it does. Class names, event handlers, forms, routes and your content are preserved verbatim, and an independent QA pass diffs every line to make sure nothing functional moved.

I'm not a developer — is this for me?

Yes. Connect with Google or GitHub and we do all the work on the code. Every change is shown in plain language with the code beside it — you just approve or reject, without touching a line of code.

Do you invent numbers, images or testimonials?

Never. It's our most important rule. If a fact is missing — a company number, a phone, a testimonial — we don't make it up; we come back to you with a question. Better an empty field than a false fact.

What about Hebrew and RTL?

That's exactly where most tools fail, and for us it's the whole point. We have dozens of signals unique to Hebrew, right-to-left and Israeli regulation — a font with real Hebrew, correct currency direction, an accessibility statement per Israeli standard 5568, and more.

Is the code still mine?

Completely. You get the upgraded site as a downloadable file or a Pull Request straight to your repo. No lock-in, no dependency on us — your code, before and after.

How much does it cost and how long does it take?

The first scan is free, no credit card. After that, full pricing for the build is shown up front based on your site's size — before you approve anything. The process runs online and takes minutes, not days.
